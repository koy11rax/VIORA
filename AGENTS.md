# Viora

Android is the first delivery target; retain Windows/Linux application runners.
The approved design and scope are recorded in docs/. Do not replace real tunnel
or authorization state with a simulated success. Development fixtures must be
explicitly enabled and labelled. Do not copy credentials, production databases,
Telegram bot tokens, subscription links or release signing keys into this repo.
Do not send real broadcasts or mutate production as part of testing.
Bulk grants must preview recipients, require a reason, authorize on the server,
commit atomically, audit changes and prevent repeated credit.
